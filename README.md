# Fungal Industry Map

An open map of the organizations that build with fungi, in food, ingredients, materials, health, agriculture, spawn and growing, and tools. Each entry gives the organization's status and links to a source that a reader can open.

Open the map: `index.html`, or the GitHub Pages address of this repository.

## What is in it

- 579 organizations. Each one has a status: active, acquired or new owner, collapsed, sold off or paused, or award winner.
- 155 investors, each tied to a named fungal company through a funding announcement.
- Short sections on closed companies, assets that could be kept for the field, and what the field cannot measure yet.

The scope includes yeasts and filamentous fungi used for fermentation, because yeasts are fungi.

## Data

The same data is in two files that anyone can open in a spreadsheet:

- `data/organizations.csv`: name, segment, status, tag, note, source.
- `data/investors.csv`: investor, fungal companies backed, new fungal investment since 2024.

## How I check entries

Each change of status (acquired, closed, sold, paused, renamed) comes from a page that I opened: a regulator or company register, a company announcement, or a news report. Each active entry links to a source that shows the organization exists and what it does. I did not confirm current operations for every active entry.

"Status unconfirmed" means that the organization still has a website or a listing, but I found nothing dated in the last 12 months. It does not mean that the organization closed.

I update the map each month. The history of this repository shows each change and its date.

## Corrections

If an entry is wrong or out of date, open an issue on this repository with the correct fact and a link to a source. If you worked at one of these companies, you can also write to me privately by replying to any email from my newsletter. One corrected date or number is enough.
